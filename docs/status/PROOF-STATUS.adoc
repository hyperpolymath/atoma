== Vocabulary

Policy definitions from `standards/3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc`, carried here so
the words used in this file mean one thing. They are not statements about Atoma's
proofs.

* *Status token* (§5): `proven`, `proven-with-postulates`, `tested-only`,
  `argued`, `open`.
* *PWG* (§8), always written with its prefix, never as a bare "Grade":
** `PWG-3`: the Agda proof is `proven` or `proven-with-postulates` under the
   canonical §2.1 invocation, `agda --safe --without-K --cubical-compatible`,
   and no recheck leg counts yet;
** `PWG-2i`: one recheck leg also counts (Rocq or Lean);
** `PWG-2ii`: both legs count;
** `PWG-1`: `PWG-2ii`, plus every counted witness (Agda, Rocq and Lean) has a
   current receipt from a headless, isolated, pinned checker run on the exact
   source commit;
** `ungraded`: a theory row below `PWG-3`, or any row with no receipt yet, an
   `open` row included, whether it is a theory theorem or an engineering
   obligation;
** `n/a`: not on the ladder once it has a result: a test, a refusal fixture, an
   assumption, or a discharged engineering proof in Idris2, SPARK, Creusot or
   Verus. §8.1 puts these off the ladder. An `open` engineering obligation is not `n/a`: an `open` engineering
   obligation stays `ungraded` until it has a receipt.
* *Recheck legs* (§2): `transported`, `re-proved`, `waived` or `unavailable`,
  each with its §2.2 correspondence state, for example `BRIDGE-PENDING` or
  `RECONCILED`. A leg counts towards a grade only when it is `RECONCILED`.

A grade is derived from receipts, not asserted (§8.4). Name the receipt in the
table, and a claim that has none stays `ungraded`. `PWG-3` is not a discharge:
a theorem at `PWG-3` still owes both legs.
